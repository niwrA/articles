How the three systems cooperate

A capability standard, a learning pathway and a verification record serve distinct functions.

1 · CAPABILITIES
Define a meaningful outcome

Atomic enough to assess, reusable across occupations, with versioned dependencies and distinct legal requirements.

2 · LEARNING
Choose a viable route

Prior knowledge, guidance, independence, peer teaching, practical projects, and spaced transfer inform the route.

3 · EVIDENCE
Prove the claim

Gather appropriate authentic demonstrations, evaluate validity and reliability, and use formal accreditation only where earned.

Conceptual research and software demonstration only.

All learner evidence is fictional and stored only in the running browser session. Simulated evidence buttons do not issue qualifications. The 1F/2F curricular tags are unverified domain hints, not a claim of meeting official standards. AA/RA designations require independently verified professional education, supervised practice and register entry. There is no live labour-market data and no AI service behind this demo.
